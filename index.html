About

Built for more

I’m Chris Garland. I live on Long Island, I work with my hands, and I’m building something bigger than myself.

By day I’m a Fire Alarm Technician: inspections, testing and troubleshooting on life-safety systems. Honest work that actually matters. By night I’m the founder of Garland Enterprises, the umbrella for everything I’m creating.

I care about the businesses I work for like they’re mine, because when I’m in, I give all I got, all the time. Every job, every shift, whatever it takes. And I’m honest about the other side of that: if the work isn’t rewarded or appreciated, I leave, and I build my own. That’s how Garland Enterprises started.

James Madison University, B.A. Communications Studies, Class of 2024.
